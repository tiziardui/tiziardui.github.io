Associate Professor of Econometrics · University of Rome Tor Vergata

My research lies primarily in microeconometrics, with applications in health, education, and labor economics. A central theme of my work is understanding how peer effects, social comparisons, and social environments shape individual choices, behaviors, and outcomes.
